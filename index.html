Scene I The Atelier

We don't just plan events. We design experiences.

Twelve years, four hundred celebrations, one obsession: the feeling in the room. We take a small number of families each season so that nothing is delegated and nothing is repeated.

Thodupuzha is our base, and most of what we build sits within a few hours of it: church and temple weddings across Idukki, receptions in Kochi, hill venues above Munnar. We design, plan and produce end to end, from concept, styling and vendors to the timeline and the quiet choreography guests never see.
